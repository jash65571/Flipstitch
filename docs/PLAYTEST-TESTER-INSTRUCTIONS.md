# For the player

*Read this aloud, or hand it over. It is the whole script.*

---

Thanks for helping us with this.

This is **FlipStitch**, a small puzzle game for phones. It is not finished, and
we want to find out how it feels to someone seeing it for the first time.

**We are testing the game, not you.** There are no wrong answers here. If
something is confusing, that is the game's problem to fix, and it is exactly
what we are hoping to learn.

Please just play it the way you normally would.

If you are comfortable, **say what you are thinking out loud** — what you are
looking at, what you expected to happen, what you are trying. It helps a lot.
If you would rather play quietly, that is fine too.

I am going to stay quiet while you play. That is on purpose, not rudeness — if I
explain things, we stop learning anything. So if you get stuck, **have a go
anyway**. There is nothing you can break, and nothing you can lose.

You can stop whenever you like, for any reason, and you do not have to give one.

At the end there are a few short questions. You can skip any of them.

Before you start, the game will show you a short note about what it records
while you play. Have a read, and choose whichever option you prefer — the game
plays exactly the same either way.

That's it. Whenever you're ready.
